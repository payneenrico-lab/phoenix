# Phoenix

A browser remake of the 1980 arcade shooter *Phoenix*. One `index.html`, no build step: smooth vector graphics drawn at your screen's resolution, Web Audio sound, keyboard and touch controls.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

| Action      | Keyboard              | Touch   |
|-------------|-----------------------|---------|
| Move        | ← → (or A / D)        | Slider  |
| Fire        | Space (or Z / ↑)      | FIRE    |
| Force field | ↓ (or S / Shift)      | SHIELD  |
| Pause       | P / Esc               | PAUSE   |
| Sound       | M                     | MUTE    |

The force field lasts about a second, stops you moving and firing, and needs five seconds to recharge. Anything that touches it is destroyed.

## Waves

1. **The Flock** – small birds in a chevron that peel off and dive at you.
2. **The Flock Returns** – faster, more aggressive birds.
3. **Phoenix Eggs** – eggs hatch into big firebirds. Shoot their wings off, but only a hit to the body kills them, and the wings grow back.
4. **Firebirds** – a faster, swoopier brood.
5. **The Mothership** – chip through the orange hull and the rotating purple conveyor to hit the alien pilot. The quicker the kill, the bigger the score (1000–9000).

The cycle then repeats at a higher difficulty. You start with 3 ships and earn an extra one at 5,000 points and every 30,000 after that.
